About

Hi, I'm Sienna.

I work at the point where design decisions and working code meet — turning a rough idea into something people can actually use, and enjoy using.

My process is simple: understand the problem, sketch it until it's clear, then build it properly. No wasted steps, no unnecessary complexity.

Outside of client work, I spend a lot of time looking up — literally. Most of my best layout ideas come from staring at the sky longer than I probably should.

"Design is the first impression. Code is the lasting one."
